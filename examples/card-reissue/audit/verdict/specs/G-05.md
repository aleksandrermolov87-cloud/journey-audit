# G-05 · P1 · A relative knows on one screen what they may do for the cardholder

## Problem
The frame names a relative acting for an elderly parent, but the map has no path or policy for representatives. Persona p3 (synthetic) asked "Is it even mine to do this?".
Finding: EDGE-05 (high, med, absent). Most of the fix depends on owner decision 5.

## Goal and metric
- Relatives in a test who name the allowed action within 1 min · now: not measured · target: ≥ 4 of 5 · how: usability test (validation task 7).
- Sessions opening "Acting for someone else?" that end in an allowed route (block, call) · target: ≥ 90 % · how: `representative_info_opened` → `card_blocked` or `hotline_call_tapped`.

## Scope
- s2 block dialog → link "Acting for someone else?".
- New info screen with the policy and routes.
- Out of scope: power-of-attorney flows and account access for third parties (adjacent journey).

## TO BE journey (fragment)
| Step | User does | Sees | Response | Undo / way back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Taps "Acting for someone else?" | What a relative may do, what only the cardholder may do, how to call | — | Back |
| 2a | Taps "Call 24/7 block line" | Dialer | — | Back |

## States
| State | What the user sees | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Policy in 3 lines with routes | Call, go back |
| Logged in as cardholder (helped by a relative) | Note: "You can block it here yourself" | Return to dialog |

## UI copy
- Link: "Acting for someone else?"
- Heading: "Helping a family member"
- Lines (default option, **needs legal review**): "You can ask us to block their card by phone, 24/7." / "Only the cardholder can order a new card or change the address."
- Button: "Call 24/7 block line"

## Acceptance criteria
- Given the block dialog, When it renders, Then "Acting for someone else?" is visible.
- Given the user opens it, When the screen shows, Then it states what a relative may and may not do in at most 3 lines.
- Given the user taps call, Then the dialer opens with the block line.

## Analytics
`representative_info_opened` · `hotline_call_tapped{source=representative}`.

## Test cases
- Link → info → call.
- Negative: relative tries to change the address in the cardholder's session → the address change follows owner decision 5.
- Accessibility: the info screen works at 200 % font size without horizontal scroll.

## Do not break
- The main path for the cardholder gains no extra step: the link is secondary.

## Owner decisions
- Owner decision 5: may a relative block, order or change the address? Options: phone only; in-app with confirmation from the cardholder; formal power of attorney.

## Dependencies and effort
Policy and legal copy. **S** for the screen; the policy itself is outside design.
