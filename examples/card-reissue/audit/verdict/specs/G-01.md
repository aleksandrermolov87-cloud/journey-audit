# G-01 · P0 · After blocking, the user can pay today

## Problem
The JTBD is "nobody can spend my money **and** I keep paying today". The map ends on a home screen with no card at all, 5–7 days before the new one arrives (s10, emotion −1). Owner vision V2 (virtual card) has no step. All three synthetic personas finished `partial` here.
Findings: CJM-01 (critical, provided), CJM-03 (high, absent), EDGE-02 (critical, provided), LOAD-07 (medium, absent). Label: **business / content** — the goal changes the offer, not only a screen.

## Goal and metric
- Share of permanent blocks followed by a virtual card added to a phone wallet within 10 min · now: 0 % (no step) · target: ≥ 60 % (revise after 4 weeks of baseline) · how: `virtual_card_added_to_wallet` / `card_blocked{type=permanent}`.
- Share of block confirmations that show the pay-today option · target: 100 % · how: `pay_today_offer_shown`.

## Scope
- s5 confirmation → adds "Pay today" with a virtual card offer.
- New step: virtual card issued and added to the phone wallet.
- s10 → home tile shows the active virtual card.
- Out of scope: the physical card order (G-04, G-08), disputes of past charges (P2).

## States
| State | What the user sees | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Offer under the confirmation | Get the card, skip |
| Loading | "Creating your virtual card…" with expected time | Wait, leave (card arrives in app) |
| Error | "We could not create the virtual card. Your old card is still blocked." | Try again, order a physical card |
| Wallet unsupported | "Your phone does not support a wallet. Use the card details for online payments." | Show details |
| Refused (account limits) | Reason in plain words and what to do | Contact support |

## UI copy
- Block heading: "Pay today"
- Text: "Get a virtual card now. It works in your phone wallet and online until the new card arrives."
- Buttons: "Get a virtual card" · "Add to phone wallet" · "Not now"
- Done: "Ready to pay with your phone."
- **Needs legal review:** any statement about limits, fees or validity of the virtual card.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a permanent block succeeded, When the confirmation opens, Then "Pay today" is visible without scrolling on a 375×667 screen.
- Given the user taps "Get a virtual card", When issuance succeeds, Then "Add to phone wallet" appears in the same flow.
- Given issuance fails, When the error shows, Then it states the old card remains blocked and offers retry.
- Given the user skips, When they later open home, Then the offer is reachable from the card tile.
- Given a virtual card is active, When the user opens home, Then the tile shows it as ready to pay.

## Analytics

## Test cases
- Permanent block → offer shown → card issued → added to wallet → home tile shows it.
- Negative: issuance error → message says old card still blocked → retry works.
- Negative: phone without wallet → card details shown, no wallet button.
- Temporary block → offer not shown (old card can be unblocked).
- Accessibility: screen reader announces "Pay today" section and button names; works at 200 % font size.

## Do not break
- s5 relief: "card is blocked" stays first; the offer sits below it.
- Owner vision V2 becomes covered by steps 5a–5b.

## Owner decisions
- Owner decision 1: can the bank issue an instant virtual card after a permanent block? If no, the goal becomes "the user knows how and when they can pay" and the verdict becomes Redesign.
- Fee, limits and validity of the virtual card.

## Dependencies and effort
Card issuing API, wallet provisioning, legal copy. **L**: may need new backend and wallet integration.
