# G-04 · P1 · Fee and delivery date are known before the order form

## Problem
The reissue fee and "delivery in 5–7 days" appear for the first time on the order summary at confirmation (s8). Owner vision V3 asks for a delivery date before confirming. The s8 dip (emotion −1) is "a week without a card, and a fee nobody mentioned".
Findings: CJM-05 (high, provided), CJM-04 (high, med, provided), LOAD-02 (high, med, provided), EDGE-13 (medium, synthetic).

## Goal and metric
- Orders where fee and delivery date were shown before s6 · now: 0 % · target: 100 % · how: `reissue_terms_shown` before `reissue_form_opened`.
- Drop-off on the summary (s8) · now: not measured · target: −30 % from baseline · how: `reissue_summary_shown` without `reissue_confirmed`.

## Scope
- s5 → "Order a new card" shows fee and "arrives by <date>".
- s8 → repeats the same values; nothing new appears on the summary.
- Out of scope: the fee policy itself and the delivery commitment (owner decisions 2, 3).

## States
| State | What the user sees | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Fee and date on the entry and summary | Order |
| Date unavailable | "Arrives in 5–7 working days" with earliest date | Order |
| Fee waived | "Free" | Order |
| Values changed between s5 and s8 | Changed value highlighted with a note | Confirm or go back |

## UI copy
- Entry: "Order a new card" / "<fee> · arrives by <weekday day month>"
- Summary rows: "Fee", "Arrives by", "Delivery address"
- Waived: "Free for lost or stolen cards" — **needs legal review**
- Changed: "The delivery date has changed to <date>."

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a permanent block, When the confirmation shows "Order a new card", Then fee and delivery date are on the same element.
- Given the user reaches the summary, When it renders, Then fee and date equal the values shown at s5 or are highlighted as changed.
- Given no exact date is available, When the entry renders, Then it shows a range with the earliest calendar date.
- Given the fee is zero, Then the entry says "Free", not "0".

## Analytics
`reissue_terms_shown{fee, delivery_date, place=entry|summary}` · `reissue_form_opened` · `reissue_summary_shown` · `reissue_confirmed` · `reissue_terms_changed{field}`.

## Test cases
- Entry and summary show the same fee and date.
- Date changes between entry and summary → highlighted.
- Negative: delivery service unavailable → range shown, no empty field.
- Accessibility: fee and date are read as text, not only as an icon or color.

## Do not break
- s8 summary stays as the final check (useful friction).

## Owner decisions
- Owner decision 2: fee rule for lost or stolen cards.
- Owner decision 3: concrete date vs range; needs a logistics commitment.

## Dependencies and effort
Fee and delivery-estimate API, copy review. **M**: needs a delivery estimate before the form.
