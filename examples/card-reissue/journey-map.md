# Journey map: reissue a lost debit card (AI draft)

Generated by an AI assistant for a mobile banking app. Kept as the "source" of the example audit.

## Who and why

A commuter who just lost a wallet. Usually on the phone, often on the move. Wants to be sure nobody can spend the money, and to keep paying for things today. Does not want to visit a branch.

## Journey

| Stage | User does | Sees | Response | Undo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Notice | Opens the app | Home screen with balance and card tile | — | — |
| 2. Block | Taps the card tile, opens card settings | Card screen with 9 actions | — | Back |
| 3. Block | Taps "Block card" | Dialog: block temporarily or permanently? | Spinner while the provider blocks the card | Cancel |
| 4. Block | Reads the confirmation | Card status BLOCKED_PERM, reissue available | — | — |
| 5. Reissue | Taps "Order a new card" | Form: delivery address, card design, name on card | — | Back |
| 6. Reissue | Types the delivery address | Address field with suggestions | — | — |
| 7. Reissue | Confirms the order | Summary with fee and delivery in 5–7 days | Push notification from the issuer | — |
| 8. Done | Closes the app | Home screen without a card tile | — | — |

## Errors

If blocking fails, the app shows a generic error.

## Owner's vision (product brief)

- V1 Blocking takes one tap from the home screen.
- V2 The user can pay today with a virtual card.
- V3 Delivery date is shown before the user confirms.
